Shared World

Every depth is one dungeon. Monsters, loot, and other players are real. First to the stairs wins the race down.

Classic Roguelike

Turn-based. Unidentified potions. Hunger. Permadeath. If you know NetHack, you already know half the controls.

Play Anywhere

Browser, telnet, or WebSocket client. No install. Jump in, pick a name, die horribly within minutes.
